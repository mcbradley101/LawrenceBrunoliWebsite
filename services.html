WHAT WE DO

Construction Expertise.
Built Around Your Project.

From project development and design through construction, Lawrence Brunoli, Inc. brings experience, coordination, and clear communication to five primary services.

01

GENERAL CONTRACTOR

The role of a General Contractor is to assume the responsibility of constructing all or selected phases of a project.

Lawrence Brunoli, Inc. knows that a well-managed project supports a high-quality product and client satisfaction. The company uses construction management software alongside its experience and knowledge to efficiently control project activities.

Coordinating the work

  • Scheduling and managing construction activities.
  • Controlling project costs.
  • Coordinating construction trades and subcontractors.
  • Managing the flow of materials and equipment to the jobsite.
  • Maintaining a safe work environment.

Established relationships with skilled, experienced subcontractors support consistent performance. Lawrence Brunoli, Inc. maintains relationships with subcontractors whose past work has met the company's expectations, recognizing that their work reflects on the company.

02

CONSTRUCTION MANAGEMENT

Construction management is a service whereby an owner obtains the assistance of a construction expert to act as their agent in administering the construction process.

As Construction Manager, Lawrence Brunoli, Inc. prepares budgets and schedules to help the owner understand project costs and time frames, assists with design-team selection, and provides contract interpretation.

From planning to construction

  • Soliciting and evaluating bids after construction documents are completed.
  • Selecting qualified general contractors or subcontractors, considering both the effectiveness of the work and cost efficiency.
  • Providing project supervision when required.
  • Coordinating construction schedules, activities, and subcontractors.
  • Reviewing work in place and accepting or rejecting it as required.
  • Recording and processing project reports and site documentation.
03

DESIGN-BUILD

Under a design-build contract, Lawrence Brunoli, Inc. provides the design services and also carries out the construction activities.

The company works with the owner and design team to bring their visions together in an efficient design that meets the owner's functional and aesthetic expectations.

Connecting design and construction

  • Consulting during project development.
  • Providing a design team to meet the owner's needs.
  • Coordinating the owner, design team, and contract documents.
  • Carrying out construction activities.

Lawrence Brunoli, Inc. works with an established core of experienced architects and engineers to provide quality designs and professional service.

04

CONSTRUCTION CONSULTING

Construction Consulting is an advisory function which can be provided at any phase of the design or construction process.

Clients draw on the company's construction experience and knowledge to make timely, cost-effective decisions and gain a clear understanding of the construction process.

Informed decisions. Practical recommendations.

  • Conducting an in-depth review of existing construction documents.
  • Identifying constructability concerns.
  • Evaluating potential high-cost areas.
  • Identifying labor and material availability concerns.
  • Identifying possible scheduling conflicts.
  • Recommending ways to avoid or correct the identified issues.
05

OWNER REPRESENTATION

As an Owner’s Representative, Lawrence Brunoli, Inc. will act as a knowledgeable and experienced intermediary between the client, the architect and the building contractor.

A HISTORICAL COMPANY ACHIEVEMENT

Company Recognition

U.S. SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

Administrator's Award for Excellence

Danbury Federal Correctional project

Lawrence Brunoli, Inc. received the U.S. Small Business Administration's Administrator's Award for Excellence in connection with the Danbury Federal Correctional project.

The company's historical material states that:

  • It was the only award given to a general contractor in the United States.
  • The award criteria involved projects under $17 million with restrictive work areas.
  • The project was completed four months ahead of schedule.

For review: the award-exclusivity and criteria claims have not been independently verified.
