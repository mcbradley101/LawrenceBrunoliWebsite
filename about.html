Lawrence J. Brunoli, Jr.

Owner, President, Treasurer, and Co-Founder

lbrunoli@lbrunoli.com

Lawrence J. Brunoli, Jr. has served as Owner, President, Treasurer, and Co-Founder of Lawrence Brunoli, Inc. since its inception in 1987.

Responsibilities

His responsibilities include project administration, purchasing, bidding, estimating, and project management from bid through completion.

  • Planning construction activities, developing schedules, directing employees, and coordinating subcontractors and material suppliers.
  • Reviewing plans and specifications, preparing clarifications and price estimates, conducting field inspections, and troubleshooting.
  • Negotiating with subcontractors and suppliers and maintaining correspondence with owners, architects, engineers, and project partners.

Peter Gavin

Senior Project Manager/Scheduler

Experience & responsibilities

Peter joined Lawrence Brunoli, Inc. in September 2001. His responsibilities cover every phase of project management, from startup through closeout.

His industry experience includes heavy highway and bridge construction, Amtrak's Northeast Rail Electrification Project, power plants, and commercial buildings. His specialties include Primavera project scheduling, cost control, and the technical understanding needed for complex projects.

Education

B.S. in Construction Management, Wentworth Institute of Technology, 1989.

Career highlights
