Recognition

U.S. Small Business Administration — Administrator's Award for Excellence

On June 10, 1999, Lawrence Brunoli, Inc. received the Administrator's Award for Excellence from the U.S. Small Business Administration. The award recognizes the company's outstanding contribution and service to the nation through its work supporting the federal procurement system.

Award Transcription

U.S. Small Business Administration

Administrator's Award for Excellence

Presented to

Lawrence Brunoli Inc.

In recognition of outstanding contribution and service to the nation by a small business in satisfying the needs of the Federal procurement system.

June 10, 1999

Signed by the Administrator.

Connecticut Secretary of the State — Corporate Certification

On October 26, 2009, the Connecticut Secretary of the State issued this official certification documenting Lawrence Brunoli, Inc.'s corporate standing and original incorporation filing dated January 20, 1987. The certificate reflects the company's longstanding presence as a Connecticut corporation.

This historical certificate describes the records as of October 26, 2009; it does not establish present-day corporate status.

Certificate Transcription

Office of the Secretary of the State of Connecticut

I, the Connecticut Secretary of the State, and keeper of the seal thereof,

DO HEREBY CERTIFY, that the certificate of incorporation of

LAWRENCE BRUNOLI, INC.

a domestic STOCK corporation, was filed in this office on January 20, 1987, a certificate of dissolution has not been filed, the corporation has filed all annual reports, and so far as indicated by the records of this office such corporation is in existence.

Secretary of the State

Date Issued: October 26, 2009

Business ID: 0193668

Express

Certificate Number: 2009244582001
