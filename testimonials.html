STATE OF CONNECTICUT
DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS

Bruce L. Morris
Commissioner

June 21, 1991

To Whom This May Concern:

As the Director of Construction for the State Department of Public Works, I have had the pleasure of working with Lawrence Brunoli, Inc. over the last three (3) years. As is typical of all construction projects, the General Contractor is only as good as the quality of the contract documents. In Brunoli Incorporated's case, where the documents were of excellent to good quality, the contractor did an excellent job. Where the documents were of less than good quality, Brunoli Inc. was very much at the mercy of the Architect/Engineer of record. In all cases, Brunoli Inc., made every attempt to work out problems so as to finish the project in a successful manner for all parties concerned.

I would be pleased to have Lawrence Brunoli Inc. on any State project for which I was in charge.

Sincerely,
